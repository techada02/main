How It Works
Simple 4-step process to get work done
1

Post a Job

Describe the work, select category, add your location and budget. Mark "Urgent" for priority dispatch.

2

Workers Get Notified

Top 10 nearby workers are notified instantly – via app or SMS on keypad phones. First to accept wins.

3

OTP-Verified Start

Worker arrives, you share a start OTP. Job begins securely. Completion also requires an OTP.

4

Rate & Pay

After completion, rate the worker. Build digital history. Fair, dispute-free payments.

📞 No Smartphone? No Problem!

KaamMitra supports keypad/basic phone workers through SMS confirmation.

  • Customer posts job → Backend finds nearby workers
  • Keypad phone worker receives SMS: "New job: Mason needed near MG Road. Reply YES to accept."
  • Worker replies YES or 1 to confirm
  • Backend locks job, customer notified instantly
  • No internet, no app needed — just a working SIM card
